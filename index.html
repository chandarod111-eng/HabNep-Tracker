About HabNep

HabNep

HabNep is a simple and modern habit tracking app designed to help you build consistency and track your daily progress.

What you can do

  • Create and manage daily habits
  • Track completed habits
  • Take rest days without losing progress
  • View weekly, monthly and yearly analytics
  • Switch between A.D. and B.S. (Nepali Date)
  • Backup and restore your data
